## 摘要

The cause of Housing and Urban-Rural Construction is a crucial underpinning for the national economy, people's livelihood, and high-quality development. Its transformation and upgrading toward industrialization, digitalization, and greenization are highly aligned with the development requirements of "new-quality productive forces". This process urgently calls for a large-scale, well-structured, and high-caliber contingent of inter-disciplinary, innovative, and compound scientific and technological talents. This paper systematically sorts out the development status and classification system of high-level scientific and technological talents in China's housing and urban-rural construction field from three levels: top strategic scientists, national-level leading and core talents, and young scientific and technological talent echelons. It analyzes three core challenges currently faced by the development of scientific and technological talents in this field: the structural mismatch between talent supply and the new demands of the industry, the disconnection between the talent evaluation system and the innovation laws of the industry, and the inadequate industry-academia-research-application collaborative talent cultivation mechanism. Correspondingly, the paper puts forward three suggestions to support the high-quality development of scientific and technological talents in the housing and urban-rural construction field: establishing a modern talent evaluation system oriented toward new-quality productive forces, deepening the reform of classified evaluation and industry-education integration, and optimizing innovation platforms and development environments.
